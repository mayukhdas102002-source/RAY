# 11 · Genre playbooks

Short, practical moves per genre. They sit on top of the pipeline. For deep per-model phrasing, use the `library/` routes in `SKILL.md`. (The notes on hooks, timeline segmentation and genre structures were informed by studying the higgsfield-seedance2-jineng collection. They're written here in original form. See `CREDITS.md`.)

## Universal: the first two seconds

Open on something that forces a look, then earn it:
- a problem already on screen (a queue, a binder, a SOLD sign),
- scale surprise (macro of a familiar object, or a tiny figure in a huge space),
- a sound contrast (silence, then one sharp sound),
- a clear human want on a face.

For TV-style spots the hook can be gentle. For social it must land by 1.5s. Never open on a slow establishing shot with no tension.

## Timeline shapes

- **4–5s beat:** hook, then one action, then a held payoff frame.
- **8–10s beat:** hook, context, escalation, payoff.
- **15s spot:** hook (0–2), want (2–5), turn with the product (5–10), payoff (10–13), end card (13–15).
- **30s spot:** hook, want, first proof, second proof, promise lands, payoff, end card.
- **45–60s film:** add a middle "engine" (floors, hours, bids) and one mid-film breath before the payoff.

## Fintech, banking and lending

- The emotional currency is **relief and control**. Show the burden shrinking (the binder never opened, hold music cut off).
- The product moment is a real screen: one headline, one number, one button. "Approved." is the strongest word in the category.
- Show competition or speed through a human ritual (an auction, a queue, a lift ride), not dashboards.
- Keep the wit kind to the incumbents. Charming rivals, never villains.
- Compliance: mark vision claims, use fictional lenders, and avoid anything that reads as personal advice.

## Real estate and property

- **Space is a character.** Arrival (exterior with a gate or path), threshold (a door opening), the heart room (the lounge in window light), the view.
- Moves that sell space: a steadicam walk-in from behind the buyer, a threshold reveal through a doorway, a slow push toward a window view, a vertical tilt from floor texture to ceiling light, a drone arrival ending at the front door.
- Light: golden hour outside, soft window light inside, practicals on. Avoid flat midday.
- Local specifics beat generic luxury: the weatherboard villa, the tin roof, pōhutukawa, the letterbox.
- Real-estate wow: a doorway that opens onto something impossible but emotionally true (a lift onto a front lawn).

## Brand story and vision

- Arcs that work: problem to solution, customer transformation, day in the life, future vision (a museum of today's pain), before and after.
- One protagonist and one want. A founder VO only if it's genuinely theirs and short.
- For investor films: end on scale (the network, the country, the number of homes) after the human payoff, never instead of it.

## SaaS and app motion design

- The UI is the hero, but real. Remotion screens with springs, count-ups and state ticks. Use one accent colour.
- Hook ideas: a number counting up, a messy state snapping clean, a device entering from darkness.
- Keep live-action and UI in the same world: the screen sits in a hand in a real room, not floating in a void, unless the brand's style is abstract.

## Product and e-commerce

- Hero product moves: a slow orbit, a macro texture pass, a drop into frame with real weight, a hand-use demonstration with consequence (the lid unscrews, the fabric creases).
- Pattern: hook, showcase, benefit in use, price or offer, CTA, in about 15s.

## Social hook (9:16)

- Face or problem in the first frame, and text-safe areas top and bottom.
- Pattern breaks every 2–3s (an angle change, a sound hit, a reveal).
- Deliver a :06 and a :15 from the same plates.

## Food and beverage

- Appetite equals texture plus steam plus sound. Macro with backlight, slow pours at a real speed, and the crunch or fizz audible.

## Fashion

- Walks are the hardest stunt: keep them short and shoot from behind or waist-up. Sell fabric movement with wind and backlight.
