# Worked example: teardown of seven Zillow spots

Measured with `tools/cut_log.sh` and `tools/contact_sheet.sh`. The ads are summarised in our own words, for analysis.

| Spot | Length · shots · avg | Story | The wow | Product moment | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| "Let's get you a home loan." | :30 · 9 · 3.3s | People at home tap phones and the world physically reacts | Magical realism, about 1s each, in real rooms | The phone in every scene, then the logo system | Surreal works as a brief joke about speed, never as a long metaphor |
| "Look what you can Zillow now" | :15 · 9 · 1.7s | A man lugging files; a listing card floats; a spotlight; a new flat | One floating UI card and one lighting switch | The brand as a verb | Paperwork pain fits in one prop |
| "Ah, the feeling…" (Pool) | :30 · 11 · 2.7s | A man floats underwater; his partner taps "You're pre-approved!" | Underwater stillness = relief | One screen for 2s, then one line | Sell the feeling with one instant metaphor |
| Get Pre-Qualified | :30 · 8 · 3.75s | A tap at the kitchen table, then the camera flows into the future home | One continuous move where time jumps | One button, one line | Show the after straight after the tap |
| Financing Solutions | :15 · ~10 | A house montage, a phone, a couple in bed | Editing rhythm | One screen, one line | 15s = rhythm + one screen + one line |
| "Someday Starts Today" | :15 · 12 · 1.25s | A family's someday; the phone reads "Your loan has been approved" | None, just honesty and pace | The approval notification as the climax | The approval notification is the category's strongest shot |
| Compare Homes | :90 · 23 · 3.9s | A couple pool-hops strangers' pools at night like a heist | Genre play | Revealed at 1:23 | A funny, true buyer insight carries long-form |

## The seven rules we took
1. One real person, one real moment. 2. One wow, brief. 3. The phone is the hinge. 4. One screen, 3–5 words. 5. Show the after. 6. Two lines of copy at most. 7. Warm practical light, lived-in homes, real casting.
