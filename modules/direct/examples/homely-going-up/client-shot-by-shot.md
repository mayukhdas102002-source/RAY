**Homely · "Going Up" (54.5-second concept film)**

**Logline**
A couple take the lift up to their home-loan appointment. By the time the doors open, Homely has done the whole loan on the way up, and the doors open onto their new home.

**The idea in one line**
With Homely, getting a home loan takes about as long as a lift ride.

---

**1. The lobby (0:00–0:03.5)**
Aroha and Josh walk into an Auckland office building, a little dressed up and nervous. Josh carries a thick folder of paperwork.
*Message:* The old way: appointments, paperwork and stress.

**2. Level 24 (0:03.5–0:06)**
The lobby screen reads "Level 24 · Home Lending · 9:00 · A. Ngata & J. Reid."
*Message:* They're on their way to a bank appointment.

**3. Going up (0:06–0:09)**
They step into the lift and the doors close. A calm voice says "Going up."
*Message:* The journey starts. The lift's voice is Homely.

**4. Connect (0:09–0:13)**
Aroha opens Homely and connects her bank. Documents check themselves, and her borrower profile and spending appear instantly.
*Message:* **Real-time borrower profile.** No forms and no paperwork.

**5. Uber Eats (0:13–0:15.5)**
Josh reads her screen: "We spend how much on Uber Eats?"
*Message:* Homely shows your real spending, instantly.

**6. Level five (0:15.5–0:18)**
Ding. "Level five. You're pre-approved with nine lenders."
*Message:* **Instant eligibility and lender matching.**

**7. Ask the lift (0:18–0:22)**
Josh asks, "Can we afford Grey Lynn?" The lift answers: "You can. Fix for two years and you'll save thirty-eight thousand."
*Message:* **Conversational AI and AI financial advice.**

**8. Level nine (0:22–0:25)**
A loan officer steps in, already reading their application on her tablet.
*Message:* **Banks are connected to Homely.**

**9. The file (0:25–0:27)**
Her tablet shows their Homely application in the bank's system: verified and ready.
*Message:* **Open Finance integration.**

**10. Level eleven (0:27–0:30)**
A second lender steps in: "We'll beat that rate."
*Message:* **Lenders compete for you.**

**11. A day in a lift (0:30–0:38)**
Through the glass wall, a whole day passes over Auckland while they hold hands.
*Message:* The wait happens outside the window; inside, it's calm.

**12. Level 24 (0:38–0:40.5)**
"Level twenty-four. You're approved." The phone reads **Approved.**
*Message:* **A lending decision within 24 hours.**

**13. The look (0:40.5–0:42.5)**
They look at each other. Josh lowers the folder he never opened.
*Message:* Relief.

**14. Doors opening (0:42.5–0:47)**
The doors open onto sunshine, a front path, a pōhutukawa and their new villa.
*Message:* The wow: Homely takes you straight home.

**15. Moved in (0:47–0:50.5)**
Movers bring a sofa. The phone shows 10% off furniture and $2,500 cashback. Josh: "The lift paid for the couch."
*Message:* **Product marketplace and rewards.**

**16. End card (0:50.5–0:54.5)**
**"Approved before the doors open."** · **Homely · Going up.**
