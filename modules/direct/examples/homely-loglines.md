# Worked example: concept loglines (Homely, NZ AI home loans)

Six directions pitched after the client asked for "mind-blowing, hyper-real, award-level" ideas. They show the logline format in `templates/logline-sheet.md`.

1. **The Museum of Waiting** ⭐ — In 2027 the old home loan is a museum exhibit: "The 47-page application, c. 2024", preserved hold music, a stuffed bank manager's desk. The last exhibit is a phone on a plinth. *Line: Home loans as you know them are history.*
2. **Going Once** ⭐ — A classic Kiwi auction, but the lot is the first-home buyer and the bidders are banks, bidding the rate down. *Line: Make the banks bid for you.*
3. **1974** ⭐ — Nana and Grandad got their loan in an afternoon with a handshake. Match cuts across 53 years show Aroha doing the same in 2027. *Line: Home loans used to take an afternoon. Now they do again.*
4. **One Take, One Day** — A single 60s oner across 24 hours of ordinary life while the loan happens in the background. *Line: Your life doesn't stop for a home loan.*
5. **The Dog Knew First** — The dog is packing before the couple finish applying. *Line: Approved before the dog finished packing.*
6. **Slice of Heaven** — Approvals across the country start a national sing-along (licensing needed). *Line: Home, for every Kiwi.*

The client ultimately chose **Going Up** (see `homely-going-up/`), a lift ride where each floor is a feature, level 24 means 24 hours, and the doors open onto the new home.

## What was rejected, and why (useful as anti-patterns)

- **"Overnight / paper cranes."** Paper towers turned into origami birds, and a room turned into a bank hall. Rejected as too dramatic and too many metaphors, with the product shown only as symbols.
- **Early "Going Up".** Rejected for unclear motivation (why are they in the lift?) and for shots that were too short and felt stitched. Fixed with a lobby directory set-up and a stretch from 45s to 54.5s.
