# 13 · Industry notes (add to any playbook)

What changes by sector. These are production cautions, not legal advice: for regulated content, flag the person's compliance/legal review and log it in `DECISIONS.md`. The Direct module's `director/11-genre-playbooks.md` has deeper creative patterns for fintech, real estate, brand story, SaaS, product/e-commerce, social hooks, food and fashion.

| Sector | Creative notes | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|
| **Finance, banking, lending, insurance, crypto** | Relief and clarity over hype; real customers' moments; show the app as the hinge | Rates/returns need disclaimers and dates; no guaranteed outcomes; "not financial advice" where applicable; risk warnings; regulator rules per market |
| **Health, wellness, pharma, medical devices** | Human, calm, evidence-led; clinicians only if real and consented | No unapproved health claims; before/after rules; patient privacy; prescription-drug ad regulations; no AI "doctors" presented as real |
| **Property / real estate** | The life in the home, light and space; neighbourhood moments | Don't misrepresent the property (no AI-added features, views or rooms); label virtual staging; listing data accuracy |
| **Travel, hospitality, tourism** | Sensory detail, the one feeling of the place; local voices | Don't show what guests can't actually experience; Indigenous/cultural content with permission and correct language; drone rules |
| **Food & beverage** | Texture, steam, sound; hands making; the first bite | Alcohol rules (age-gating, no appeal to minors, responsible messaging); nutrition/health claims; allergens if claims are made |
| **Fashion & beauty** | Movement, fabric, attitude; real skin | Retouching/filters disclosures in some markets; model releases; no body-altering AI on real people without consent |
| **SaaS / tech / B2B** | Real UI, real speed, one aha per video; customer proof | No fake UI; feature availability honest; security/compliance claims sourced; logos of customers only with permission |
| **Education** | Learner-first, accessible, plain language | Student/minor consent; accessibility (captions, transcripts) usually mandatory; accuracy review |
| **Government / public sector** | Plain language, inclusive casting, multilingual | Accessibility standards (captions, AD, contrast), impartiality, political-period restrictions, approvals chain |
| **Nonprofit / charity** | One person's dignified story, clear ask, outcomes | Beneficiary consent and dignity; donation-claim accuracy; no poverty/pity framing; child safeguarding |
| **Automotive / mobility** | Motion, roads, freedom; real vehicle details | Safe-driving depiction rules; spec/price disclaimers; closed-course notes for stunts |
| **Gaming / entertainment** | Gameplay truth, hype edits, character IP | Show real gameplay or label "not actual gameplay"; age ratings; licensed music |
| **Retail / e-commerce** | Product in hand, price/offer clarity, urgency without pressure tactics | Price/offer accuracy and terms; "was/now" rules; influencer/ad disclosure |
| **Politics / advocacy** | Clear, sourced arguments | Paid political ad rules and disclaimers; no synthetic depictions of real candidates or officials; no deceptive civic content |
| **Personal brands / creators** | Personality, consistency, series formats | Sponsorship disclosure (#ad/paid partnership); music licensing on business accounts; consent from people filmed |
