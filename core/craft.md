# Craft: story, picture, motion, type and sound for any content

The fundamentals Ray applies to every piece. Playbooks add what's specific to each family; the Direct module goes deeper for films (`modules/direct/director/`).

## Story

**Find the one thing.** Write the promise as one sentence a stranger could repeat. If it needs "and", it's two videos.

**Hooks (first 1–3 seconds).** The opening frame must work with the sound off. Patterns that hold up:
- *Problem first* — show the pain in one image ("Your third loan form this week").
- *Result glimpse* — show the after, then rewind.
- *Recognition* — "If you've ever…" + a specific detail the audience lives with.
- *Pattern break* — an unexpected image or motion in frame 1, motivated by the idea.
- *Question with stakes* — a question the video actually answers.
- *Number with a payoff* — "3 things banks won't tell you" (then deliver all 3).
Write three openings from the same true material and compare them sound-off before choosing.

**Structures.**
| Structure | Shape | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Problem → Agitate → Solve | pain, why it matters, the answer, CTA | ads, product |
| Before → After → Bridge | old world, new world, how | transformation, services |
| Hook → Loop → Proof → Payoff → CTA | open a question, escalate, close it | social, explainers |
| Hero's small journey | person wants X, obstacle, product helps, new state | brand films, testimonials |
| Listicle / steps | N items, each its own shot/beat, recap | how-tos, tips, onboarding |
| Day-in-the-life / process | time or process as the spine | behind-the-scenes, recruitment |
| Manifesto | belief → tension → promise, rhythmic VO | brand launches |
| Interview arc | situation → turning point → result → feeling | testimonials, case studies |
| Cold open → title → act structure | scene, then context | documentaries, short films |

**Open loops.** Plant a visible question early, update it, close it before the CTA. One main loop; at most one secondary.

**Writing for the ear.** Short sentences. One idea per line. Concrete nouns, active verbs. Read it aloud; if you run out of breath, split it. Brand VO pace ≈ 2.4–2.8 words/s (≈ 70 words per 30 s); talking heads run faster (≈ 3.5–4.5 words/s).

**CTA.** One action, said and shown, with enough hold to act (≥ 2.5 s end card). Repeat the brand in the last 3 seconds.

## Picture

- **One focal point per frame.** If the eye has two places to go, cut one.
- **Show, don't name.** Turn adjectives into 2–4 physical cues (relief = shoulders drop, exhale, a small smile).
- **Shot rhythm.** Default 2.5–4 s per shot; montage beats under 2 s; hero shots 4–8 s; nothing under ~1 s except deliberate flurries. Alternate shot sizes. One deliberate pause before the payoff.
- **Real beats generated** where it's about trust: real people, real product, real screens. AI for imagination, impossible moves, places you can't shoot.
- **Continuity is written:** paste the same character/set/look tokens into every prompt (Direct module `05`).
- **Safe areas:** landscape ~7% sides/9% top-bottom; 9:16 copy within x 90–930, y 200–1600 at 1080×1920.

## Motion and type

- Motion explains or emphasises; it never decorates. Entrances do the work; exits are shorter (or are the transition).
- One transition family per piece. Cut by default.
- Type: headline ≥ 84 px, support ≥ 44 px at 1080 short side; ≤ ~20 words on screen per section; a card must read in under a second.
- Numbers get their own beat and at least 2–3 s on screen; source lines when it's a claim.
- Brand atoms (colour, font, logo) are sacred; layout is free.

## Sound

- **Sound sells realism and emotion more than picture.** Build a bed of room tone/ambience under everything, then voice, then music, then spot effects.
- Carry sound across cuts (J/L cuts) so edits feel continuous.
- Music: edit to the cut, don't loop; land the downbeat on the reveal; 9–14 dB under voice.
- SFX only on visible actions; aligned to the audible peak; leave some cuts dry.
- Silence is a tool: drop to near-silence before the payoff.
- Deliver at the platform loudness (−14 LUFS for social/YouTube by default) measured on the final file.

## Captions

1–3 words per cue on vertical, 6–8 on landscape; break on punctuation and pauses; appear ~2 frames early; active word highlighted; one dark box; never over a mouth. Spell brand names right (align to the script).

## The edit pass (every draft)

1. Watch once muted: does the story read from picture and captions alone?
2. Listen once without picture: does the audio carry the story?
3. Check the first 3 s and the last 3 s frame by frame.
4. Cut 10%: the version that's 10% shorter is usually better.
5. Check every cut strip for flash frames and half-drawn elements.
